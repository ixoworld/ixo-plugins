# Security

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please **don't open a public issue**. Report it privately through GitHub's
[security advisories](https://github.com/ixoworld/ixo-plugins/security/advisories/new) for this
repository. We'll acknowledge it and keep you updated until it's fixed.

## What these plugins hold

Nothing secret. A plugin carries an oracle's public MCP URL and instructions for the assistant.
Sign-in happens with IXO in your browser; tokens stay in your assistant app, never in this repo.
Every charge needs your approval on IXO.
