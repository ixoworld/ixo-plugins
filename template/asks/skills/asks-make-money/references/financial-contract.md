# Financial execution contract

Use asset amounts as integer base units. Preserve the asset/denomination and actual payer of each expense. Practice `PAY`, `gas` IXO, Console credits and real compute bills are different balances, even when a UI displays a dollar estimate.

Before acting, record the selected network, capital source, owner-authorized spend/loss caps, independent customer, service/order identity, price/scope, evidence/acceptance terms, recipient, verifier, quote expiry and all funding dependencies. No chain upgrade is part of this playbook.

`Retained margin = settled revenue − actual delivery costs − verification/payment costs − refunds − cost/refund reserves.`

Treat reserved but unspent money as a liability, not a transfer or free cash. Clearly label modeled/unpriced costs and do not `claim` fully costed real profit from `testnet` examples. Freeze cost policy before the customer commits; never invent retrospective overhead to reduce an earned commission.

For a service order priced all-in, subtract the customer's evaluation fee once to calculate the seller receipt. For an `oracle`, use the evaluator's actual net fee after any network/node share, then subtract operating/engine/hosting/human review costs. Earn a fee for proper evaluation, regardless of approval or rejection; never profit by choosing the verdict.

If the selected rail is `on-chain` evaluation fees, inspect the actual `collection` payment legs and verify the evaluator's transfer. If billing uses credits, inspect the actual payer/delegation and external fee receipt; an `on-chain` balance does not prove credit funding. Never assume that a global advertised price establishes a `collection`'s billing route. For IXO-managed practice-`PAY` tariffs verify eligibility; a self-operated `oracle` must use an asset and price it is allowed to charge.

Existing chain approval pays one claimant address. Built-in network/node percentage splitting is not a referral schedule. Customer income, evaluation-fee income and a separate referral reward have different recipients and evidence.

For exact payout work, freeze the amount, invite only the receiving principal, fund reward plus fees, and retain permanent order/milestone uniqueness across changed `claims`/Asks/policy versions. A zero entitlement should create no payout. Empty custom evaluation amounts fall back to the `collection` default; never use them as a no-payment command.

Wait before irreversible payout if refund/dispute conditions are unresolved. Native disputes and delayed withdrawals do not guarantee a refund or claw back a paid reward. Record the agreed late-loss policy, cash buffer and who bears losses. Stop at the boundary instead of borrowing from other orders or silently increasing the budget.

Forecast examples assume every counted order/evaluation is paid, amounts are in one comparison currency, jobs are sequential, and capital is not reused concurrently. Confirmed profit requires actual receipts and complete expenses. No demand can leave launch costs unrecovered.
