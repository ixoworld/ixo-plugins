# Pay for trying

An extra on an ask: people get some money just for trying, not only when they win.

- **"Pay for sending an answer"** (`pay_on_submit`): everyone who sends an answer gets it, good or bad.
- **"Pay if it's turned down"** (`pay_on_reject`): someone whose answer is turned down still gets it,
  so trying is never a total loss.

- **Offer it when:** the work takes real effort (research, a long write-up, going somewhere), or the
  person wants more people to try. Don't offer it for a quick photo or a one-line answer.
- **Money:** both are **set aside** when the ask is posted, for every place, on top of the reward. Say
  it the way the preview does: "$5 for each approved answer, plus $1 to each person who sends an
  answer. $12 set aside for 2 places." What isn't paid comes back when they close it.
- **Open asks:** if anyone can answer, anyone who sends *something* gets "pay for sending an answer",
  even a bad answer. The preview says so. **Say** it once in plain words, and offer "only people I
  invite" or "people I approve" ([private.md](private.md)) if they'd rather choose who can try.
- **Not with:** paying by card, a company's ask, or a company paying through its own payouts. The tool
  refuses those; say "Pay for trying only works on an ask you pay for yourself."

**Do:** `post_ask` as in [post-an-ask.md](post-an-ask.md), plus `pay_on_submit` and/or `pay_on_reject`
in dollars. Preview first, repeat the total, `confirm` on their yes, then the link to tap.
