# A private ask

## Do this

1. **Ask:** "Only people you invite, or anyone who applies and you say yes?"
2. Ask who to invite: @names, addresses, or how many private links.
3. Follow [post-an-ask.md](post-an-ask.md) with `access` (`"invite"` or `"apply"`) and `invite` (the
   @names and addresses, plus `"link"` once per private link).
4. Before they confirm, **Say** once: "Only you, the people you invite and whoever checks it can open
   this ask or read its completions. It's not on the board or Google."
5. After posting: give each person their own invite link, never the ask's page link.

- **"Only people I invite"** (`access: "invite"`): not on the board; only the invited can complete it.
- **"People I approve"** (`access: "apply"`): on the board; people apply with a note and the person
  says yes or no to each (the `invite` skill).
- Anyone can be invited: an @name, an address, or a one-person link. No LinkedIn needed.
- Money is the same as an ask: the pay goes to the person in full, the AI check is free, and what
  isn't paid comes back when they close it. Repeat the preview's total.
- App testing by verified pros → a Pro Ask ([review-job.md](review-job.md)) instead.
- Someone completing a private ask may get an Allow link first: pass it on and wait.

"Private" means four things; say which: a private ask (this); a private pro page (the `profile`
skill); a private link (one invite link for one person); keeping a completion private (the Allow link).
