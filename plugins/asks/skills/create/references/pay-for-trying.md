# Pay for trying

## Do this

1. Say the line below. 2. Post a normal ask on their choice ([post-an-ask.md](post-an-ask.md)).

An extra on an ask: people get some money just for trying, not only when they win ("pay for sending a
completion", or "pay if it's turned down").

This can't be set up from here yet. **Say:** "Paying people just for trying can't be added to an ask
from here yet. I can post it with a reward for each approved completion instead, or make the reward a
little higher so it's worth trying." Then, on their choice, [post-an-ask.md](post-an-ask.md). Never pass
`pay_on_submit` or `pay_on_reject`.
