# A private ask

An ask only chosen people can answer. Two ways, said exactly like this:

- **"Only people I invite":** not on the board; only the people they invite can answer (`access: "invite"`).
- **"People I approve":** on the board; people apply with a note and the person says yes or no to each
  (`access: "apply"`).

- **Pick it when:** their team, their customers or named people should answer, or the answers must stay
  private. Also to hire one particular person for work that isn't an app review: invite their @name.
- **Not when:** anyone may answer → an ask ([post-an-ask.md](post-an-ask.md)); verified pros trying an
  app → a pro ask ([review-job.md](review-job.md)), which is invite-only or open to any verified pro
  with its link.
- **Who answers:** anyone they invite: an @name (someone with a page), an address, or a
  one-person link. No LinkedIn needed.
- **Money:** as an ask: **set aside** when posted, what isn't paid comes back when they close it. Repeat
  the preview's total; if it names a share asks.world keeps, say it as the tool does, never a number from
  memory. Real money and practice money both.
- **Judged:** the same as an ask; "I check each answer myself" works here too.
- **Privacy:** only the person, the people they invite and whoever answered or checks it can open an
  "only people I invite" ask or read its answers. Anyone else sees a sign-in page; it is not on Google
  or the board. Its receipt is private too. **Say** this once before posting. To bring someone in, send
  their **invite link**, not the ask's page link (that one only shows strangers a sign-in card).

**Do:** `post_ask` as in [post-an-ask.md](post-an-ask.md) with `access` and `invite` (@names, addresses,
or `"link"` once per link). Later invites, links and applications: the `invite` skill. An answerer on a
private ask taps an Allow link first: pass it on and wait.

**"Private" means four things.** Say which: a private ask (this); a private pro page (only people they
share it with can invite them: the `profile` skill); a private link (one invite link for one person);
keeping an answer private (the Allow link above).
